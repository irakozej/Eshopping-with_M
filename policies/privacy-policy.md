# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **Beyond Beauty Boutique** (Beyond Beauty Ltd, RDB 122456686), based in Kicukiro, Kigali, Rwanda, handles your personal information when you shop with us.

## Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

- **Name** — so we know who the order belongs to
- **Email address** — for your account and order updates
- **Phone number** — to coordinate delivery and payment
- **Delivery address** — to deliver your order
- **Order history** — the products you have bought from us

We only collect what we need to process and deliver your orders.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Process your orders and take payment
- Deliver your order or arrange in-store pickup
- Send you order confirmations and delivery updates
- Respond to your questions and handle returns
- Keep records required to run our business

We do **not** sell your personal information to anyone.

## Service providers (processors)

To run the store, we share the minimum necessary information with trusted providers who act on our behalf:

- **Mobile money payments** — we use **MTN Mobile Money** to take payment. Payment details you enter are handled by MTN to complete the transaction.
- **Email delivery** — we use an email service provider to send order confirmations and updates to your email address.

These providers may only use your information to provide their service to us, and are expected to protect it.

## How long we keep your information

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements.
