# Return Policy

**Beyond Beauty Boutique** (Beyond Beauty Ltd, RDB 122456686) wants you to love what you buy. If something isn't right, here's how returns work.

## Return window

You may request a return within **24 hours of delivery**. After 24 hours we are unable to accept a return, except where the law requires otherwise.

## Condition of items

To be accepted, items must be:

- **Unworn and unwashed**, in their original condition
- With **all tags still attached**
- In their **original packaging**

We cannot accept items that show signs of wear, are missing tags, or are damaged after delivery.

## How to start a return

Contact us within the 24-hour window and have your **order number** ready:

- **WhatsApp:** [+250 794 803 462](https://wa.me/250794803462)
- **Email:** [beautybeyond706@gmail.com](mailto:beautybeyond706@gmail.com)

We'll confirm whether your item qualifies and explain how to return it to us in Kicukiro, Kigali.

## Refunds

Once we receive your returned item and confirm it meets the conditions above, we will issue your refund within **5 to 7 business days**. Refunds are made to your original payment method (for example, MTN Mobile Money).

Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (for example, a wrong or faulty item).

## Exchanges

If you'd like a different size or colour, start a return as above and place a new order for the item you want, subject to availability.

## Questions

For any questions about a return, reach us on WhatsApp or by email above.
